## What I understood
- A KPI is not just a query that displays information; it must have a clear business definition and purpose.
- KPIs help measure progress toward specific goals and make performance easier to track.
- Edge cases should always be considered when designing KPIs to ensure the metric remains reliable and meaningful.
- SQL aggregation functions are often used as the technical foundation for calculating KPIs.
